2. Van Gogh Self portrait (REAL 2)
  Description:
    ​Name of the painting:  Self-Portrait (often referred to as Self-Portrait, 1889 or Portrait de l'artiste)
    ​Name of painter: Vincent van Gogh
    ​Timeline: Post-Impressionist era (Saint-Rémy-de-Provence period) 
    Start Date: Late August / Early September 1889 
    Completion: September 1889
      
    ​Brief Explanation About the Painting
    Painted shortly after recovering from a severe breakdown at the Saint-Paul asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, this masterpiece is widely considered one of Van Gogh's final and most intense self-portraits. The painting features the artist dressed in a suit coat against a backdrop of swirling, wavy blue and green lines (arabesques). The dynamic, chaotic energy of the background and his hair contrasts sharply with his motionless posture and piercing, pale gaze. Van Gogh created this piece as an intense study of emotional recovery, seeking to capture his inner mental state through vibrant colors and expressive brushwork rather than strict photographic realism.

    Place: Seattle, Washington 
    Source: https://lik.com/products/infinity-tree
     The piece captures the dramatic autumn transformation of a moss-covered Japanese Maple tree nestled in the woods of Seattle, Washington
    
    Overview & Creative Process
    • The Shot: Using a wide-angle lens, Lik captured a brilliant canopy of vibrant red and orange autumn leaves contrasted against a heavily textured, mossy trunk.
    • The Patience: Lik famously camped out and waited 10 days in the Pacific Northwest wilderness for the shifting temperatures and rainfall to peak, providing the perfect saturation of color.
    • The Exposure: The final image was secured during a single second of serene stillness.
